FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are these official exam questions?

No. These are practice questions written by PermitPal AU, not official questions from any licensing authority. PermitPal AU is an independent study aid — it is not affiliated with, representative of, or endorsed by any Australian government or licensing authority.

Is PermitPal AU free?

Yes, the app is free to download. Your first mock exam and the first 10 practice questions per state or territory are free to try. After that, a single one-time purchase unlocks unlimited mock exams and the full practice question bank forever — no subscription.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. All study content and question banks are bundled into the app at install time and work fully offline — even in airplane mode.

Is my study data safe?

Yes. Your progress, saved questions and exam results are stored only on your own device — no server stores or tracks this data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I switch states after I've started studying?

Yes. You can change your state or territory anytime in Settings without losing the progress saved for your previous region.

Is the content kept up to date?

Yes. We update the question banks and study content whenever the official state and territory road-rule sources change.
